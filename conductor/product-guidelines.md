# Product Guidelines

-   **Prose:** direct, plain, second person to the agent ("You MUST…") in
    skills; first person and conversational in the README.
-   **Skills are protocols:** numbered steps, explicit HALT points, explicit
    user-confirmation points. Prefer precise wording over cleverness.
-   **Keep the fork small:** remove before adding. Anything added must earn its
    place and must not be required for the core skills to work.
-   **Visual identity (for any UI):** calm, dense, dark-first with a light
    theme; one accent colour; status colours used only for status.
